BRS-CIPD-001	An Index/Target Response of PD is derived based on a single lesion instance, and not on the lesion sums.
BRS-CIPD-005	In order to have a response of PD, an individual lesion (node or extranodal) must meet the following three (3) conditions:
1.	The lesion must be considered abnormal (the lesion must have an LDi of > 15 mm).
2.	The lesion must have a ≥ 50% increase in PPD from nadir.
Note: If nadir PPD is 0 and PPD at current time point is numeric and non-zero then condition 2 is considered true.
3.	If LDi or SDi of the lesion at nadir time point is ≤ 20 mm, it must have increased by 5 mm from nadir LDi or nadir SDi.
OR
If LDi or SDi of the lesion at nadir time point is > 20 mm, it must have an increased by 10 mm from nadir LDi or nadir SDi.
Note: Nadir time point for a lesion is the time point with lowest PPD of all prior time points. Condition 3 should be met for at least LDi or SDi. (LDi is compared to LDi, SDi is compared to SDi)
Note: for split lesions, the sums of LDi/SDi (PPD) should be included when compared to nadir, not individual values.
Note: for lesions that merge, the application must add the PPD of the two (or more) lesions that have merged at the current time point and all prior timepoints to determine the nadir time point of the combined lesions, when calculating PD response. 
•	When lesions merge and lesion is considered abnormal (#1) and the 50% increase (#2) is met the LDi or SDi increase (#3) is not required to derive PD. 
o	LDi and SDi increase (#3) will no longer apply to the lesions in all subsequent time points once merged regardless if they are no longer merged.	PD
